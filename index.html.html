01 · Welcome

Can Emma plan a profitable month?

Emma owns a small coffee shop. She wants to forecast sales, control expenses, protect cash, and measure performance.

Today you will

Prepare a sales budget, operating expense budget, cash budget, and budgeted income statement.

You will also

Calculate and interpret a simple budget variance to recommend a management action.

02 · Explain

How a master budget fits together

A master budget combines connected plans for operations and financial results.

Sales budget

Forecasts revenue from expected units and selling price.

Operating expense budget

Forecasts costs of running the business.

Cash budget

Forecasts when cash comes in and goes out.

Budgeted income statement

Forecasts profit based on revenue and expenses.

Cash budget

Ending Cash = Beginning Cash + Cash Receipts − Cash Payments
Emma begins with $1,500. She expects $5,000 in cash receipts and $4,200 in cash payments.
What is Emma’s budgeted ending cash ($)?

06 · Learn + practice

Budgeted income statement

For this simplified example, Emma budgets:

Sales revenue$6,000
Cost of goods sold($1,800)
Operating expenses($2,600)
Budgeted Operating Income = Sales − Cost of Goods Sold − Operating Expenses
Calculate budgeted operating income ($).

07 · Learn + practice

Budget variances

A variance is the difference between actual and budgeted performance. Whether it is favorable depends on the item.

Emma budgeted $300 for utilities. The actual utility expense was $380.
By how many dollars did utilities exceed the budget?

Is this expense variance favorable or unfavorable?
